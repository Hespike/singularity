Wall Crawler

Type: Talent (Generic)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 2

Description

You can scale walls and obstacles with greater ease than most, finding handholds and footholds that others miss. Your movements are fluid and instinctive, allowing you to traverse vertical surfaces that would challenge others.

Effect

You gain a climbing speed of 15 feet. You can climb without making ability checks on surfaces that would normally require them, as long as you have one hand free to grip holds.

Notes

  • You must have at least one hand free to use your climbing speed.
  • While climbing, you suffer the same penalties as other climbing creatures. See Restricted Ranged Combat for details.

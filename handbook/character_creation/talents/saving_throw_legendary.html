Saving Throw Training (Legendary)

Type: Talent (Saving Throw)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 16
  • Saving Throw Training (Masterful)

Description

Your character’s resilience is absolute. They possess a mythic level of grit and presence of mind that makes them virtually unshakable in the face of disaster.

Effect

Choose one saving throw in which your character is Masterful. Your proficiency in that saving throw increases to Legendary.

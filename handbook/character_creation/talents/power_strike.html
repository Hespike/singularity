Power Strike

Type: Talent (Generic)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 2

Description

You've mastered channeling your strength into devastating melee strikes. When you put your full force behind an attack, your blows carry significantly more destructive power, leaving enemies reeling from the impact.

Effect

Before making a melee weapon attack, you can choose to spend 1 additional energy beyond the normal attack cost. If you do, you add +2 to your damage roll for that attack only. This effect applies only to the next attack you make, and can be used with any melee weapon attack, including unarmed strikes.

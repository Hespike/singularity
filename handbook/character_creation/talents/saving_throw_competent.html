Saving Throw Training (Competent)

Type: Talent (Saving Throw)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 6
  • Saving Throw Training (Apprentice)

Description

Through repeated exposure to danger and refined instinct, your character’s ability to resist harmful effects becomes a professional standard.

Effect

Choose one saving throw in which your character is Apprentice. Your proficiency in that saving throw increases to Competent.

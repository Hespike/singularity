Handless Climber

Type: Talent (Generic)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 8
  • Wall Crawler

Description

You've developed techniques that allow you to scale surfaces using your feet, knees, and even minimal contact points, freeing your hands for combat or other tasks.

Effect

You no longer need a free hand to climb.

Notes

  • While climbing, you still suffer the same penalties as other climbing creatures. See Restricted Ranged Combat for details.

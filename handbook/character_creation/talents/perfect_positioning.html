Wall Runner's Flow

Type: Talent (Generic)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 16
  • Handless Climber

Description

Your mastery of three-dimensional movement has reached the absolute peak. You understand combat flow at an instinctive level, constantly repositioning yourself through walls, ceilings, and vertical surfaces in ways that make your attacks impossible to predict. Like a spider or urban free-runner at the height of their art, you flow through space in a perpetual dance, striking from every conceivable angle without losing momentum or precision. Each attack comes from a new angle, each strike is as fresh as your first.

Effect

You ignore the Repeated Attack Penalty (RAP) completely. When you use the same attack source multiple times in a single turn, you do not suffer any penalty to your attack rolls or Difficulty Class (DC), regardless of how many times you repeat the attack. Your constant repositioning through vertical movement makes each attack as unpredictable as the first.

Notes

  • This is the pinnacle of the climbing mastery talent tree, representing your ability to use three-dimensional movement to maintain perfect combat positioning at all times.
  • Your mastery of walls, ceilings, and vertical surfaces allows you to attack from angles that defy normal combat expectations.

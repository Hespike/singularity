Public Image II

Type: Talent (Social)

Requirements

  • Prime Level 4
  • Public Image I

Description

Your reputation has grown significantly, and your name and image are widely recognized within your community and beyond. You are a household name, and civilians not only recognize you but actively seek you out or remember you from broadcasts, news, or word of mouth. Your influence extends further, and people are eager to cooperate or assist you in their own interest.

Effect

You gain +10 to Charm-based checks when interacting with civilians who recognize you or are aware of your public identity.

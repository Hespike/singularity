Military

Description

You served in the armed forces, whether in active combat, logistics, or support roles. Military training instilled discipline, tactical awareness, and the ability to function under pressure. You understand chain of command, weapon systems, and how to work as part of a team in high-stakes situations.

Benefits

  • Ability Boost: Increase your Might, Endurance, or Wits ability score by +1 (choose one).
  • Military Training: You gain Apprentice training in Athletics (Might), Survival (Wits), or Technology (Wits) (choose one).

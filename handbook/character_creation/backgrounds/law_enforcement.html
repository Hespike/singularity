Law Enforcement

Description

You served as a police officer, detective, federal agent, or security officer. Your training emphasized investigation, observation, and maintaining order. You understand criminal behavior, legal procedures, and how to read situations and people. Whether patrolling streets or solving complex cases, you've developed sharp instincts and the ability to defuse tense situations.

Benefits

  • Ability Boost: Increase your Wits or Charm ability score by +1 (choose one).
  • Law Enforcement Training: You gain Apprentice training in Investigation (Wits), Insight (Wits), or Intimidation (Charm) (choose one).

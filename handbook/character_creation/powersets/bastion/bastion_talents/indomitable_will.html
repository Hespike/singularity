Indomitable Will

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 10

Description

Your mental fortitude matches your physical resilience, allowing you to resist effects that would control or disable you.

Effect

You have advantage on saving throws against effects that would cause you to be Paralyzed, Staggered, or Dazed. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus to all Wits and Charm saving throws.

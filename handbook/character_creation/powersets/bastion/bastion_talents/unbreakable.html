Unbreakable

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 20

Description

You have achieved the pinnacle of defensive mastery. Your will to survive is so strong that you can push through injuries that would fell others, refusing to fall even when your body should give out.

Effect

A number of times per day equal to your Endurance modifier (minimum 1), when you would be reduced to 0 HP and become unconscious, you are instead reduced to 1 HP and remain conscious. You do not gain a Wound from this instance of damage.

Regenerative Fortitude

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 7

Description

Your body's natural healing processes are accelerated, allowing you to recover from injuries more quickly.

Effect

A number of times per day equal to your Endurance modifier (minimum 1), you can use a free action to regain HP equal to your Bastion level. This healing cannot exceed your maximum HP.

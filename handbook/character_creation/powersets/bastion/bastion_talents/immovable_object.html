Immovable Object

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 19

Description

You become an anchor point in reality, impossible to move or displace against your will.

Effect

You cannot be moved against your will by any means, including forced movement, teleportation, or effects that would push, pull, or reposition you. You can still move voluntarily. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against effects that would knock you Prone.

Defensive Stance

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 3

Description

You adopt a defensive posture that makes you harder to hit, whether through careful positioning, shield work, or simply making yourself a smaller target.

Effect

You can spend 1 energy to enter a defensive stance. While in this stance, you gain a +2 bonus to AC. The stance ends if you move, or you can end it on your turn as a free action.

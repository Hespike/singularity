Adaptive Defense

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 14

Description

Your defenses adapt to incoming attacks, learning from each hit to better resist similar damage.

Effect

The first time you take damage of a specific type each encounter, you gain Resistance 5 to that damage type for the remainder of the encounter. This resistance stacks with other resistances you may have.

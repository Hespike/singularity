Protective Barrier

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 9

Description

You can extend your defensive capabilities to protect nearby allies, creating a zone of relative safety around you.

Effect

Allies within 15 feet of you gain a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus increases to +2 at Bastion level 15, and +3 at Bastion level 20.

Rapid Intercept

Type: Talent (Bastion)

Requirements

  • Bastion 12
  • Intercept Attack

Description

Your reflexes and defensive training allow you to intercept attacks meant for allies even at greater distances, moving quickly to take the blow yourself.

Effect

When you use Intercept Attack, you can move up to your speed to reach the ally, rather than needing to be within 5 feet already. After you reach the ally, you swap places with them. The ally moves to the space you just occupied (where you reached them), not to your original starting position.

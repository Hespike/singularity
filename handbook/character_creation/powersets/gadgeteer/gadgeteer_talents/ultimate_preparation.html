Ultimate Preparation

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 20
  • Enough Prep Time

Description

You have achieved the pinnacle of tactical preparation. Given enough time, you can analyze and counter any threat with perfect precision.

Effect

When you use Enough Prep Time, the bonuses from your preparations are doubled. This means:

  • Gain a +2 bonus per Gadgeteer level to your Gadget Tuning DC against the chosen enemy (instead of +1).
  • Gain a +2 bonus per Gadgeteer level to attack rolls made with gadgets against the chosen enemy (instead of +1).

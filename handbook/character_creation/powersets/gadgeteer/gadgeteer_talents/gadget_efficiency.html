Gadget Efficiency

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 10

Description

Your mastery of gadget design allows you to maintain your devices more efficiently, requiring less energy to keep them running.

Effect

A number of times per long rest equal to your Wits modifier (minimum 1), when you use the Maintain action, you can reduce the maintain cost by 1 (minimum 0).

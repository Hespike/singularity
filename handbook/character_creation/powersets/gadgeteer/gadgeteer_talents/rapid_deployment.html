Rapid Deployment

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 3

Description

Your gadgets are optimized for quick activation, allowing you to deploy them with minimal setup time.

Effect

When you use a gadget, you can reduce its energy cost by 1 (minimum 1 energy). You can use this ability a number of times per encounter equal to your Wits modifier (minimum 1).

Advanced Loadout

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 9
  • Expanded Loadout

Description

Your carrying capacity and organizational systems have been refined, allowing you to prepare even more gadgets.

Effect

You can prepare 1 additional Level 1 gadget slot each day. Additionally, your Expanded Loadout now grants 4 additional Level 0 gadget slots (instead of 2).

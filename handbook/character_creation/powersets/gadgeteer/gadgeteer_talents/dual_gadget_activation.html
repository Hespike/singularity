Reactive Gadgetry

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 10

Description

Your gadgets are designed for instant response, allowing you to deploy them reflexively when you need them most.

Effect

A number of times per encounter equal to your Wits modifier (minimum 1), you can use a gadget as a reaction instead of an action. You must still pay the gadget's normal energy cost, and the gadget must have a triggering condition you can react to (such as being attacked, an ally taking damage, or an enemy moving within range). You cannot use this ability with gadgets that already use reactions.

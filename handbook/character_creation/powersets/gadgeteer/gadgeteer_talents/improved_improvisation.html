Improved Improvisation

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 5
  • Improvised Gadget

Description

Your ability to create gadgets on the fly has improved, allowing you to craft more powerful devices in the heat of battle.

Effect

When you use Improvised Gadget, you can create a Level 1 gadget instead of a Level 0 gadget. The energy cost remains 2.

Gadget Arsenal

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 17

Description

Your preparation and organization allow you to maintain a vast array of gadgets, ready for any situation.

Effect

You gain additional gadget slot value equal to half your Gadgeteer level, rounded down. Each gadget costs a number of slot value equal to its level.

Examples: At level 20, you gain 10 slot value. You could prepare:

  • 10 Level 1 gadgets
  • 2 Level 5 gadgets
  • 3 Level 3 gadgets and 1 Level 1 gadget

Master Improvisation

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 19
  • Improved Improvisation

Description

Your ability to create gadgets from nothing has reached its peak. You can assemble complex devices from the most basic materials in moments.

Effect

When you use Improvised Gadget, you can create a gadget of any level up to Level 3. You can use Improvised Gadget a number of times per long rest equal to your Wits (minimum 1).

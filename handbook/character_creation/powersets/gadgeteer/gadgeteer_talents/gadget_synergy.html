Gadget Synergy

Type: Talent (Gadgeteer)

Requirements

  • Gadgeteer 14

Description

Your understanding of gadget interactions allows you to combine effects for more powerful results when actively maintaining multiple devices.

Effect

Once per long rest, when you use a gadget that deals damage while you are maintaining at least one other gadget that targets the same creature or area, the damage-dealing gadget deals double damage.

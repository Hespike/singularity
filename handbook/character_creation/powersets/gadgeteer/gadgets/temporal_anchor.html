Temporal Anchor

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You implant a temporal displacement beacon on an ally that allows them to "rewind" to a previous state, healing wounds and removing negative conditions.

Temporal Anchor

Type: Action
Range: Touch
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You place a temporal anchor on a willing creature. For the duration, once per round as a reaction when the target would take damage or be affected by a condition, they can activate the anchor to "rewind" that effect. The target immediately regains HP equal to the damage they would have taken, or removes the condition, as if the attack or effect never occurred.

The anchor can be used a number of times equal to 1 + Gadget Tuning rank before it is depleted. Once depleted, the effect ends.

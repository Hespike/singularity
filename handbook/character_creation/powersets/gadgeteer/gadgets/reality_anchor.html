Reality Anchor

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You deploy a device that stabilizes the fabric of reality in an area, preventing teleportation, dimensional travel, and other reality-warping effects while reinforcing the laws of physics.

Reality Anchor

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 10 minutes (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You create a 30-foot radius field of stabilized reality centered on a point within range. While active:

  • Teleportation and dimensional travel effects fail automatically within the field
  • Creatures cannot become incorporeal or phase through matter
  • Illusions and phantasmal effects are suppressed
  • All creatures in the field have advantage on saving throws against abilities that manipulate time, space, or reality

The anchor has 30 + Gadget Tuning rank HP. When reduced to 0 HP, the effect ends.

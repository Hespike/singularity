Utility Multi-Tool

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

A compact device that can morph into various tools—lockpicks, wire cutters, screwdrivers, and more—allowing you to overcome mechanical obstacles with precision and efficiency.

Utility Multi-Tool

Type: Action
Range: Touch
Cost: 1 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute

Effect

While the multi-tool is active, you gain a +2 bonus to skill checks involving mechanical devices, locks, traps, or similar technical challenges. This includes attempts to pick locks, disable traps, repair equipment, or interact with complex machinery.

The multi-tool can function as any standard tool for the duration, automatically adjusting its form as needed.

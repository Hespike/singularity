Quantum Lock

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You fire a specialized device that generates a localized quantum field, freezing a target in place by manipulating their molecular vibration.

Quantum Lock

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

Target a creature within range. That creature must make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature is unaffected.
  • Success: The creature is Staggered 2 for 1 round.
  • Failure: The creature is Paralyzed for 1 round, then Staggered 2 for 1 minute. At the end of each of their turns, they can attempt a new Endurance saving throw to end the Staggered effect.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature is Paralyzed for 1 minute. At the end of each of their turns, they can attempt a new Endurance saving throw to end the effect.

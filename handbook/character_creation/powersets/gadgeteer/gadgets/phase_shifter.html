Phase Shifter

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You activate a quantum displacement device that allows you or an ally to phase partially out of reality, becoming incorporeal and able to pass through solid matter.

Phase Shifter

Type: Action
Range: Touch
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You or a willing creature within range becomes Incorporeal for the duration. You can end this effect early as a free action on your turn.

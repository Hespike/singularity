Adrenaline Injector

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You administer a precise cocktail of performance-enhancing nanites and synthetic adrenaline through a rapid-injection device, dramatically boosting an ally's combat effectiveness.

Adrenaline Injector

Type: Action
Range: Touch
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute

Effect

You or a willing creature within range gains the following benefits:

  • +1 bonus to attack rolls
  • +10 feet to speed
  • +1 bonus to saving throws

When the effect ends, the target becomes Fatigued for 1 minute.

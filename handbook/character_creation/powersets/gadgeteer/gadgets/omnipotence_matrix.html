Omnipotence Matrix

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You activate your ultimate creation—a master control system that links all your gadgets, amplifying their power and granting you near-complete battlefield control.

Omnipotence Matrix

Type: Action
Range: Self
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute

Effect

You activate the ultimate integration of all your gadget systems. For the duration, you gain the following benefits:

  • All your gadget energy costs are reduced by half (rounded down, minimum 1)
  • You gain +3 bonus to AC and attack rolls
  • Your Gadget Tuning DC increases by 3
  • All damage dealt by your gadgets is maximized (roll maximum damage instead of rolling dice)

After this effect ends, you become Exhausted and cannot use any gadgets for 1 hour.

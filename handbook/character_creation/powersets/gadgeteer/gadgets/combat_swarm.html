Combat Swarm

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You release a cloud of micro-drones equipped with cutting lasers and taser probes, creating an autonomous attack force that harasses enemies.

Combat Swarm

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You create a swarm of micro-drones in a 20-foot radius centered on a point within range. The swarm persists for the duration.

When you maintain this gadget, the swarm makes a melee attack roll using your Gadget Tuning skill. All creatures within the swarm's area are affected by this attack roll. Each creature that would be hit by the attack takes 2d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Kinetic damage. You can also move the swarm's 20-foot radius area up to Gadget Tuning training × 5 feet.

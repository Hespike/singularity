Liquid Foam Spray

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You unleash a pressurized canister of quick-hardening chemical foam that coats the ground or an enemy's limbs, making movement extremely difficult.

Liquid Foam Spray

Type: Action
Range: 15 feet
Cost: 2 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 10 minutes

Effect

You target a 5-foot square. That square becomes difficult terrain.

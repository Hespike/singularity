Disruption Field

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You deploy an electromagnetic pulse emitter that creates an area of interference, disrupting electronic systems, energy weapons, and even biological functions.

Disruption Field

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You create a 20-foot radius field of disruptive energy centered on a point within range. Any creature that enters or starts their turn in the field must make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature is unaffected.
  • Success: The creature is unaffected.
  • Failure: The creature is Staggered 1 until they leave the field and cannot use any abilities or gadgets that require energy points while in the field.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature is Staggered 2 until they leave the field and cannot use any abilities or gadgets that require energy points while in the field.

Electronic devices, energy weapons, and powered armor within the field malfunction. Creatures must make the saving throw each time they enter or start their turn in the field.

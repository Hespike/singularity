Electrostatic Web

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You launch a canister that detonates mid-air, releasing conductive nanofilaments that form an electrified web, entangling and shocking enemies in a targeted area.

Electrostatic Web

Type: Action
Range: 30 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (1 energy to maintain)

Effect

You target a 15-foot burst within range. All creatures in the area must make an Agility saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature is unaffected.
  • Success: The creature takes 1d4 + Gadget Tuning rank Electricity damage and is Staggered 1 for 1 round.
  • Failure: The creature takes 2d4 + Gadget Tuning rank Electricity damage and is Immobilized for 1 round, then is Staggered 1 for 1 minute. The creature can attempt an Agility check (DC = your Gadget Tuning DC) as an action on their turn to escape, ending the Immobilized effect on a success.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature takes 3d4 + Gadget Tuning rank Electricity damage and is Immobilized for 1 minute. The creature can attempt an Agility check (DC = your Gadget Tuning DC) as an action on their turn to escape, ending the effect on a success.

The affected area becomes difficult terrain while the web persists.

Gravity Well

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You deploy a gravity manipulation device that creates an intense gravitational field, pulling creatures toward a central point and crushing them with immense pressure.

Gravity Well

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You create a gravity well centered on a point within range. The well affects a 15-foot radius area. Any creature that enters or starts their turn in the area must make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature takes no damage and is unaffected by the gravity pull.
  • Success: The creature takes half damage and is pulled 5 feet toward the center of the well.
  • Failure: The creature takes 3d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Force damage and is pulled 10 feet toward the center of the well.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature takes 5d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Force damage, is pulled 15 feet toward the center of the well, and is knocked Prone.

Creatures within 5 feet of the center take double damage from the gravity well. The area is difficult terrain, and flying creatures must land or fall.

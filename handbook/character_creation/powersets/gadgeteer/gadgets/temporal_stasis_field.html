Temporal Stasis Field

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You create a field that halts the flow of time for all creatures except yourself within a large area, effectively freezing them in place.

Temporal Stasis Field

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 round

Effect

You create a 30-foot radius field of temporal stasis centered on a point within range. All creatures other than you within the field must make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature is unaffected.
  • Success: The creature is Staggered 3 for 1 round.
  • Failure: The creature is frozen in time (treat as Paralyzed) for 1 round. They cannot take any actions, reactions, or movement. Time does not pass for them, so conditions, ongoing effects, and durations do not progress.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature is frozen in time (treat as Paralyzed) for 1d4 + 1 rounds. They cannot take any actions, reactions, or movement. Time does not pass for them, so conditions, ongoing effects, and durations do not progress. At the end of each round after the first, they can attempt a new Endurance saving throw to end the effect early.

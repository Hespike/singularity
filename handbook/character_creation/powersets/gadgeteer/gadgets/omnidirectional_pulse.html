Omnidirectional Pulse

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You trigger a massive burst of energy in all directions from a specialized device, devastating everything in a wide radius around you.

Omnidirectional Pulse

Type: Action
Range: 30-foot burst (centered on you)
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 0
Duration: Instantaneous

Effect

You release a devastating pulse of energy in all directions. All creatures other than you within the radius must make an Agility saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature takes 3d6 Force damage.
  • Success: The creature takes 5d6 Force damage and is knocked Prone.
  • Failure: The creature takes 7d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Force damage, is knocked Prone, and is Staggered 2 for 1 round.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature takes 10d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Force damage, is knocked Prone, and is Staggered 2 for 1 round.

Plasma Wall

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You project a wall of superheated plasma between two points, creating both a defensive barrier and an offensive hazard that burns anything passing through.

Plasma Wall

Type: Action
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (1 energy to maintain)

Effect

You create a straight wall of plasma up to 30 feet long and 10 feet high within range. The wall must be continuous and cannot pass through occupied spaces. The wall provides standard cover and blocks line of effect.

Any creature that enters the wall's space or starts their turn there takes 2d6 + Gadget Tuning rank Fire damage. The wall has 3 × Gadget Tuning DC HP. When reduced to 0 HP, the wall disappears.

Force Cannon

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You fire a concentrated blast of repulsor energy from a shoulder-mounted or handheld cannon, dealing significant damage and potentially knocking targets off their feet.

Force Cannon

Type: Ranged Attack
Range: 60 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Damage: 3d6 + Wits modifier Force damage

Effect

You use your Gadget Tuning skill for the attack roll. On a hit, the target takes damage and must make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The target is unaffected by the knockback effect.
  • Success: The target is pushed back 5 feet.
  • Failure: The target is knocked Prone and pushed back 10 feet.
  • Extreme Failure: The target is knocked Prone, pushed back 20 feet, and is Staggered 1 for 1 round.

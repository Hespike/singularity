Ultimate Weapon System

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You deploy your most devastating array of weapon systems—energy cannons, plasma launchers, and missile batteries that lock onto multiple targets simultaneously.

Ultimate Weapon System

Type: Action
Range: 120 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: Instantaneous

Effect

You deploy a comprehensive weapon array that simultaneously attacks up to 5 different targets within range. For each target, make a separate attack roll using your Gadget Tuning skill. Each hit deals 6d10 + Gadget Tuning rank damage of a type you choose: Fire, Cold, Electricity, or Force. You can choose a different damage type for each attack.

Each target hit must also make an Endurance saving throw against your Gadget Tuning DC. The effect depends on their result:

  • Extreme Success: The creature is unaffected by the weapon system's concussive force.
  • Success: The creature is unaffected by the weapon system's concussive force.
  • Failure: The creature is Staggered 2 for 1 round. If all 5 attacks hit the same target, that target is Staggered 3 instead.
  • Extreme Failure: The creature is Staggered 3 for 1 round. If all 5 attacks hit the same target, that target is Staggered 3 and knocked Prone instead.

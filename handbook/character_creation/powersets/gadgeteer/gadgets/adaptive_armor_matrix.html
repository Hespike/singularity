Adaptive Armor Matrix

Type: Gadget (Gadgeteer)

Description

You deploy a network of nanites that form a protective energy barrier around an ally, adapting in real-time to incoming attacks and providing comprehensive defensive capabilities.

Adaptive Armor Matrix

Type: Action
Range: 30 feet
Cost: 4 energy
Hands: 1
Duration: 1 minute (2 energy to maintain)

Effect

You or a willing creature within range gains the following benefits:

  • +2 bonus to AC
  • Resistance 10 to all damage types
  • +2 bonus to all saving throws

The armor matrix can absorb up to 50 + (Gadget Tuning rank × 5) total damage. Once this limit is reached, the effect ends.

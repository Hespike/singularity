Penetrating Shot

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 17
  • Perfect Aim

Description

Your precision has reached such a level that you can thread your shots through even the smallest gaps, completely bypassing most cover.

Effect

When you make a ranged weapon attack, you completely ignore Minor Cover and Standard Cover. Your attacks are unaffected by these types of cover.

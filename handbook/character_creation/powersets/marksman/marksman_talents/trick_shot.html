Trick Shot

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 7

Description

You can perform incredible feats with your ranged weapon, using special ammunition or unconventional techniques to achieve remarkable effects.

Effect

When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to perform a trick shot instead of dealing normal damage. Choose one of the following effects:

  • Disarming Shot: The target must make a Might saving throw against your attack roll. On a failure, they drop one held item of your choice.
  • Blinding Shot: The target must make an Agility saving throw against your attack roll. On a failure, they are Blinded for 1 round.
  • Tripping Shot: The target must make an Agility saving throw against your attack roll. On a failure, they are knocked Prone.
  • Dazing Shot: The target must make an Endurance saving throw against your attack roll. On a failure, they are Dazed for 1 round.

You can use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Agility (minimum 1).

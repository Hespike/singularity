Perfect Shot

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 20
  • Unerring Aim

Action Type

Action
Cost: 4 energy

Description

You have achieved the pinnacle of marksmanship. When you take the time to line up the perfect shot, nothing can stop it.

Effect

Make a ranged weapon attack against a target you can see. This attack automatically hits and scores a critical hit, regardless of cover, range, or other obstacles. The attack deals triple damage instead of double damage.

You can use this ability once per long rest.

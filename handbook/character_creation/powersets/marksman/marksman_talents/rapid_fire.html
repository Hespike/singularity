Rapid Fire

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 9
  • You wield a ranged weapon

Description

You unleash a flurry of shots in rapid succession, overwhelming your target with sheer volume of fire.

Effect

When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to use Rapid Fire. If you do, you make two separate ranged weapon attacks against the same target. Roll damage for both attacks normally. Combine the damage from both attacks, then apply resistance once to the combined total.

You can use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Agility (minimum 1).

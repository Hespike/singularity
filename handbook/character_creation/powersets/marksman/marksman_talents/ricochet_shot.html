Ricochet Shot

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 12

Description

You can angle your shots to bounce off surfaces, hitting targets around corners or behind cover.

Effect

When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to have the projectile ricochet off a solid surface within range before reaching the target. The ricochet allows you to attack a target that you cannot see directly, as long as you know the exact square they are in (either because a teammate pointed it out to you, or it is evident from the creature's previous movement). The attack roll takes a -5 penalty due to the ricochet.

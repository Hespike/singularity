Deadly Focus

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 17
  • Deadeye

Description

When you take the time to aim, your shots become devastatingly powerful.

Effect

When you use Deadeye, your next ranged weapon attack deals double damage on a hit.

You can use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Agility (minimum 1).

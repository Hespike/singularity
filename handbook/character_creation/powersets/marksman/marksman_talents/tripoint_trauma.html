Tripoint Trauma

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 10
  • Surgical Precision

Description

Your shots are so devastatingly precise that they shatter multiple systems at once.

Effect

When you score a Critical Hit and reduce a creature to 0 Health with a ranged weapon attack, you may choose to inflict three different Wounds instead of a single Extreme Wound.

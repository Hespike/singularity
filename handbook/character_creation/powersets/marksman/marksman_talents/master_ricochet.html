Master Ricochet

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 17
  • Ricochet Shot

Description

Your mastery of ricochet shots has reached such perfection that you can execute them with the same accuracy as direct shots.

Effect

When you use Ricochet Shot, you ignore the -5 penalty to the attack roll. Your ricochet shots are as accurate as direct shots.

Improved Deadeye

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 7
  • Deadeye

Description

Your aim has become so refined that you can maintain perfect focus even when your target moves.

Effect

When you use Deadeye, you do not lose the attack bonus if the target moves before you make the attack. You still lose the bonus if you move (unless you have Stabilized Movement).

Specialized Ammunition

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 9

Description

You have access to or can craft specialized ammunition that changes the damage type of your attacks, allowing you to adapt to different threats.

Effect

When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to change the damage type to one of the following: Fire, Cold, Electricity, Poison, or Photonic. The damage amount remains the same.

You can use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Wits (minimum 1).

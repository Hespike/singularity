Impossible Shot

Type: Talent (Marksman)

Requirements

  • Marksman 20

Action Type

Action (Cost: 4 Energy)

Description

You can make shots that seem physically impossible, threading through narrow gaps, curving around obstacles, or hitting targets at extreme ranges.

Effect

Make a ranged weapon attack against a target you can see, regardless of cover, range, or line of sight obstacles. The attack ignores all cover and has no maximum range. The attack roll takes a -5 penalty due to the impossible nature of the shot.

You can use this ability once per long rest.

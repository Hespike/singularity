Meteor Slam

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon Level 5

Description

You use your flight speed to grab or strike an airborne opponent, using your combined weight and thrust to drive them ruthlessly into the earth.

Meteor Slam

Description:
As an Action, while you are flying, you can attempt to slam a target into the ground.

Requirement:
Meteor Slam talent

Type: Melee attack
Range: Melee
Cost: 2 energy

Effect

One target, who is not on the ground(because of flying, swinging, or climbing) has to roll a Might Saving Throw against your Might DC.

  • Extreme Success: You drive the target downward a distance equal to (Your Might × 5) × 2 feet. If they hit a solid surface (like the ground), they take falling damage for the total distance traveled and are knocked Prone.
  • Success: You drive the target downward a distance equal to Your Might × 5 feet. If they hit a solid surface, they take Fall Damage for the distance traveled and are knocked Prone.
  • Failure: The target resists your momentum. They do not move.
  • Extreme Failure: You lose your flight stabilization during the struggle. You become Off-balance until the end of your next round.

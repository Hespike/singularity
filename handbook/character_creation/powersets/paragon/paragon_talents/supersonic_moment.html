Supersonic Moment

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 1

Description

For a Paragon, speed isn't just for travel; it’s the ultimate force multiplier. By accelerating to extreme velocities, you turn your body into a living projectile.

Effect

For every 15 feet you fly in before making a melee attack in the same turn, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage.

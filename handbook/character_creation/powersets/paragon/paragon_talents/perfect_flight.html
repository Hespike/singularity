Perfect Flight

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 14
  • Aerial Mastery

Description

Your flight has reached absolute perfection. You move through the air as if it were an extension of your body.

Effect

Your flying speed increases by an additional 20 feet. Additionally, you are immune to being knocked out of the air or having your flight disrupted.

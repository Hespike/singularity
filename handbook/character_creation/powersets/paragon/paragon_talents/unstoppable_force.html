Unstoppable Force

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 14
  • Breaker's Force

Description

Your momentum has become truly unstoppable. Once you begin moving, nothing can halt your advance.

Effect

You can move through any object or structure with AC 20 or lower, destroying it as you pass through. This effect works in addition to any material-based breaking abilities from Breaker's Force and its upgrades.

Worldbreaker

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 20
  • Colossal Slam

Action Type

Action
Cost: 10 energy

Description

Your power has reached such heights that you can shatter the very world around you with a single strike. This is the ultimate expression of overwhelming force.

Effect

While flying at least 200 feet above the ground, you can use an action to perform a Worldbreaker. You dive straight down up to your full flying speed, then make a melee attack against a target at ground level. If you hit, you deal your normal damage plus 12d10 additional damage. All creatures within 60 feet of the impact point must make a Might Save DC or take 8d10 kinetic damage and be knocked Prone. The ground in a 30-foot radius becomes difficult terrain, and all structures in the area are destroyed. The impact creates a crater that persists until the end of the encounter.

You can use this talent once per long rest.

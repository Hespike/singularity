Crushing Blow

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 3

Description

Your unarmed strikes carry devastating force. When you land a critical hit, the impact is truly crushing.

Effect

When you score a critical hit with an unarmed attack, you deal an additional 1d10 damage. This damage increases to 2d10 at Paragon level 10, 3d10 at Paragon level 15, and 4d10 at Paragon level 20.

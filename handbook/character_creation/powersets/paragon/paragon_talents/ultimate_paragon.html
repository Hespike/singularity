Ultimate Paragon

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 20

Description

You have achieved the ultimate expression of physical supremacy. Your body, mind, and presence have reached perfection.

Effect

You gain the following benefits:

  • Your flying speed increases by 30 feet.
  • You have advantage on all attack rolls made while flying.
  • Your unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d20 damage.
  • Once per long rest, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you can choose to remain at 1 HP instead.
  • You are immune to all conditions and cannot be moved against your will.

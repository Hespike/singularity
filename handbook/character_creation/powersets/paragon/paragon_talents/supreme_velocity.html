Supreme Velocity

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 17
  • Improved Supersonic Moment

Description

Your speed has reached truly supreme levels. You move so fast that you become a blur, striking multiple times in the blink of an eye.

Effect

When you use Supersonic Moment, you gain a +6 bonus to damage for every 15 feet you fly (instead of +4).

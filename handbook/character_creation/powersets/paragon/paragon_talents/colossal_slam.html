Colossal Slam

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 17
  • Improved Meteor Slam

Action Type

Action
Cost: 8 energy

Description

Your dive attacks have reached cataclysmic proportions. You can devastate entire areas with a single impact.

Effect

While flying at least 150 feet above the ground, you can use an action to perform a Colossal Slam. You dive straight down up to your full flying speed, then make a melee attack against a target at ground level. If you hit, you deal your normal damage plus 8d10 additional damage. All creatures within 40 feet of the impact point must make a Might Save against your Might DC or take 5d10 kinetic damage and be knocked Prone. The ground in a 20-foot radius becomes difficult terrain, and structures in the area may be destroyed at the GM's discretion.

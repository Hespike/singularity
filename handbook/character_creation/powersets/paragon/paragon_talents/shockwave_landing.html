Shockwave Landing

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 5
  • Improved Impact Control

Description

You can channel the force of your landing into a devastating shockwave that knocks nearby enemies off their feet.

Effect

When you land on a solid surface after falling at least 30 feet, you can choose to create a shockwave. All creatures within 10 feet of your landing point must make a Might Save against your Might DC. On a failure, they are knocked Prone.

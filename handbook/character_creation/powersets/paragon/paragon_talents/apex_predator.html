Apex Predator

Type: Talent (Paragon)

Requirements

  • Paragon 15

Description

Your presence alone is enough to strike fear into the hearts of your enemies. They know they face something beyond their comprehension.

Effect

While you are flying, all enemies within 60 feet of you must make a Charm Save against your Charm DC at the start of their turn. On a failure, they are Scared of you until the end of their next turn. Creatures that succeed on this save are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

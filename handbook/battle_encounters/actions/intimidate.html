Intimidate

Type: Action
Cost: 1 energy
Range: 30 feet

Description:
With a sudden shout, a well-timed taunt, or a cutting put-down, you can shake an enemy's resolve. You attempt to intimidate a creature within range that you're aware of.

Check:
Make an Intimidation skill check (or Charm check if untrained) against the target's Wits DC (or Will DC if the target has a Will save).

  • Language Barrier: If the target doesn't understand the language you are speaking, or you're not speaking a language, you take a –5 circumstance penalty to the check.

Results:

  • Extreme Success: The target becomes Scared 2.
  • Success: The target becomes Scared 1.
  • Failure: Nothing happens.
  • Extreme Failure: The target loses each Scared condition it previously gained from you.

Notes

  • On Failure or Extreme Failure, the target is temporarily immune to your attempts to Intimidate it for 1 hour.

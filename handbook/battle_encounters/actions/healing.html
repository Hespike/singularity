Healing

Type: Action
Cost: 4 energy
Range: Touch

Description:
You attempt to heal a wounded creature through medical treatment, first aid, or restorative abilities. You can choose the level of healing you attempt, with higher levels providing more healing but requiring a higher DC.

Healing Levels

When you use this action, choose one of the following healing levels. The healing amount adds your Wits modifier or Medicine skill modifier.

Level DC Base Healing
1 15 1d10 + Wits or Medicine
2 20 2d10 + Wits or Medicine
3 25 3d10 + Wits or Medicine
4 30 4d10 + Wits or Medicine
5 35 5d10 + Wits or Medicine

Check

Make a Medicine skill check (or Wits check if untrained) against the DC for the chosen healing level.

Results

  • Extreme Success: The target regains HP equal to double the healing amount for the chosen level (double the dice and double the modifier).
  • Success: The target regains HP equal to the healing amount for the chosen level.
  • Failure: Nothing happens. The target does not regain any HP.
  • Extreme Failure: The target takes damage equal to the healing amount for the chosen level (this damage cannot reduce the target below 1 HP).

Restrictions

  • This action can be used on the same target once per hour.

Notes

  • This action does not remove Wounds or Extreme Wounds, only restores HP.

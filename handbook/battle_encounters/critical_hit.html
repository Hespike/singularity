Critical Hit

A Critical Hit represents a strike that finds a gap in armor, hits a vital organ, or delivers an overwhelming amount of force.

Extreme Success

If an attack roll results in an Extreme Success defined as a total result that is 10 or more than the target's Armor Class (AC) the hit becomes a Critical Hit.

Effect

When you score a Critical Hit, you double the total damage dealt by the attack (including modifiers).

Example: If a hero with a +1 modifier to Agility attacks an enemy with AC 12 and rolls a total of 22, they have achieved an Extreme Success (+10 over AC). If the base damage was 5, the final damage dealt is 10.

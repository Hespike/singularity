Scared

You are scared of a specific creature or source of fear. The source of your fear fills you with dread, making you unable to act effectively when near it. Scared is a numerical condition with a value.

Effects:

  • You have a penalty equal to your Scared value to attack rolls and ability checks while you can see the source of your fear.
  • At the end of your turn, your Scared value reduces by 1.

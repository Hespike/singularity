Immobilized

You are stuck in place and unable to move. While Immobilized, you cannot use movement actions, but you can still take other actions normally.

Effects:

  • You cannot use the Move or Step actions.
  • You cannot move in any way, including crawling, climbing, or swimming.
  • You can still take all other actions normally, including attacks, using items, and using abilities.

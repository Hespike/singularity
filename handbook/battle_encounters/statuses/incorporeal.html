Incorporeal

You have phased partially out of reality, existing in a state between the material and ethereal planes.

Effects:

  • You can move through solid objects and creatures, but cannot end your movement inside a solid object. If you end your movement inside a solid object, you suffer cumulative Chaos damage: 1d10 at the end of your first turn, 2d10 at the end of your second turn, 3d10 at the end of your third turn, and so on.
  • You have Immunity to all damage types except Chaos damage.
  • Your attacks deal half damage to corporeal creatures, and you cannot affect objects.

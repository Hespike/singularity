Medical Treatment

Type: Downtime Action
Time: Long Rest

Description

A character with medical training can attempt to treat wounds during a long rest. This requires a Medicine skill check (or Wits check if untrained) against a DC of 15 + Prime Level. When using this action, the standard long rest healing (1 Wound) does not occur, unless the healer achieves an Extreme Success.

Results

  • Extreme Success: 1 Extreme Wound is removed after the next long rest, and also 1 Wound.
  • Success: 1 Extreme Wound is removed after the next long rest.
  • Failure: Nothing happens.
  • Extreme Failure: The character gains 1 Wound.

Notes

  • This action is performed during a long rest and replaces the standard 1 Wound healing for that rest.
  • The DC scales with the character's Prime Level, making it more challenging for higher-level characters.
  • For information on healing Wounds and Extreme Wounds through other methods, see Wounds & Dying.

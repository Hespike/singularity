Consumable

Consumables are items that provide temporary benefits or effects when used. Once consumed, these items are expended and must be replaced. They can grant abilities, resistances, or other special effects that aid heroes in specific situations or challenges.

Consumables are typically single-use items that activate when consumed, providing their effect for a specific duration or in a specific circumstance. They can be valuable tools for overcoming obstacles, surviving harsh environments, or gaining temporary advantages in combat or exploration.

Damage Type Conversion

Price: 10 credits

Description

This modification allows a weapon to deal a different type of damage than its default type. The modification includes specialized components such as photonic converters, elemental coatings, magical infusions, or technological enhancements that transform the weapon's damage output.

Effect

When you apply this modification to a weapon, choose a damage type (see Damage Types). The weapon's damage type changes from its default type (typically kinetic) to the chosen damage type.

Examples

  • A greatsword modified with a photonic converter deals photonic damage instead of kinetic damage.
  • A rifle fitted with cryo-rounds deals cold damage instead of kinetic damage.
  • A combat knife enhanced with a fire enchantment deals fire damage instead of kinetic damage.

Note: This modification replaces the weapon's original damage type.

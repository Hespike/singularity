Resistance Coating (Lesser)

Price: 15 credits

Requirement: Level 1

Description

This modification applies a specialized protective coating to armor that grants resistance to a specific type of damage. The coating can be a chemical treatment, a layer of energy-dampening material, thermal insulation, or a magical barrier infused into the armor's surface.

Effect

When you apply this modification to armor, choose a damage type (see Damage Types). The armor grants Resistance 3 to the chosen damage type. If the wearer has multiple sources of Resistance to the same damage type, only the highest value applies.

Examples

  • Combat vest coated with fire-resistant material grants Fire Resistance 3.
  • Enforcer armor treated with energy-dampening plating grants Photonic Resistance 3.
  • Riot gear enhanced with cryo-resistant insulation grants Cold Resistance 3.

Note: This modification can only be applied once per armor piece. Multiple Resistance Coatings cannot be stacked on the same armor.

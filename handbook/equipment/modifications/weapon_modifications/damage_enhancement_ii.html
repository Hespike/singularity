Damage Enhancement II

Price: 800 credits

Requirement: Level 8

Description

This advanced modification increases the weapon's damage output by adding additional damage dice. This uses refined techniques and superior materials to achieve greater destructive potential.

Effect

When you apply this modification to a weapon, its damage increases based on the weapon's damage die size:

  • Weapons with d2, d4, or d6 damage die: Add 2 additional dice of the same type. For example, a weapon dealing 1d6 damage would now deal 3d6 damage.
  • Weapons with d8 or higher damage die: Add 1 additional die of the same type. For example, a weapon dealing 1d10 damage would now deal 2d10 damage.

Resistance Enhancement I

Price: 50 credits

Requirement: Prime Level 1

Description

This enhancement is inscribed with protective sigils or contains a basic energy dampening field. It provides protection against a specific form of harm.

Effect

When you apply this modification to a suit of armor, choose a damage type (see Damage Types). The armor grants Resistance 3 to that damage type.

Note: You can apply multiple Resistance Enhancements to a single suit of armor, provided each grants resistance to a different damage type.

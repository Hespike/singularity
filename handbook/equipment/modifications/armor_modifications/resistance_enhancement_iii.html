Resistance Enhancement III

Price: 3,000 credits

Requirement: Prime Level 11

Description

This masterwork enhancement channels potent abjuration magic or employs experimental shielding technology. It offers formidable resistance against even the most intense attacks of a specific type.

Effect

When you apply this modification to a suit of armor, choose a damage type (see Damage Types). The armor grants Resistance 9 to that damage type.

Note: You can apply multiple Resistance Enhancements to a single suit of armor, provided each grants resistance to a different damage type.

Bulky

Description

This armor is exceptionally heavy and cumbersome, restricting movement even for those strong enough to wear it. While wearing armor with the Bulky trait, your movement speed is reduced by the listed amount. This penalty is applied after all other modifications to your speed.

Example

  • Bulky (5 ft.): Your speed is reduced by 5 feet.
  • Bulky (10 ft.): Your speed is reduced by 10 feet.

Small Blade Drone

Small blade drones are compact, autonomous robotic units equipped with retractable melee weapons designed for close-quarters combat. These nimble machines use flight capabilities to maneuver quickly around the battlefield, harrying enemies with swift strikes. Despite their small size and limited durability, they excel at swarming tactics and can be deployed in large numbers to overwhelm opponents through coordinated attacks from multiple angles.
